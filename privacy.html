Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 26, 2026

1. Overview

This Privacy Policy explains what information WaveLink collects when you sign in with Roblox, why we collect it, and how it's used. WaveLink authenticates users through Roblox's official OAuth 2.0 service and never has access to your Roblox password.

2. Information We Collect

3. What We Don't Collect

WaveLink never receives or stores your Roblox password. We don't collect payment information, and we don't request Roblox permissions beyond identifying you and reading public group membership.

4. How Long We Keep Data

Sign-in sessions expire automatically after 7 days. Community records persist until the group Owner or WaveLink removes them. You can end your session at any time by logging out.

5. How We Use Your Information

  • To authenticate you and display your profile within WaveLink
  • To show which of your Roblox groups have (or can create) a WaveLink Community
  • To enforce that only a group's actual Owner can create or manage its Community

We do not sell your information to third parties.

6. Third-Party Services

WaveLink relies on Roblox's public APIs and OAuth service to function. Your use of WaveLink is also subject to Roblox's own Privacy Policy. WaveLink is not affiliated with Roblox Corporation or Discord Inc.

7. Your Choices

You can revoke WaveLink's access to your Roblox account at any time from Roblox's connected-apps settings, which immediately stops WaveLink from being able to sign you in again.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Material changes will be reflected by updating the date at the top of this page.

9. Contact

Questions about this Privacy Policy can be directed to the WaveLink team through our website.
