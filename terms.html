2. What WaveLink Does

WaveLink lets you sign in with your Roblox account, view the Roblox groups you belong to, and — if you own a Roblox group — create a WaveLink "Community" for that group. Communities are intended to support moderation and session-hosting features for group owners and their staff.

3. Roblox Account Sign-In

WaveLink uses Roblox's official OAuth 2.0 sign-in. We never see or store your Roblox password. When you sign in, we receive:

  • Your Roblox user ID, username, display name, and avatar image
  • The list of Roblox groups you belong to and your rank in each

You can revoke WaveLink's access at any time from your Roblox account's connected apps settings.

4. Creating a Community

Only the confirmed Owner of a Roblox group — verified directly against Roblox's own records at the time of creation — may create a WaveLink Community for that group. Once created, the group's top two non-Owner ranks are, by default, granted permission to manage certain Community settings; the Owner may adjust this.
